THE THREE RULES THAT MATTER MOST
If anything below conflicts with these, these win. The picture is going to be shrunk into a
16 x 19 pixel box, and the character itself ends up about 13 x 19 pixels inside it. These
three are what decide whether it survives that.

1. SHAPE: the character must be SHORT AND STOCKY. Its total width must be 0.65 to 0.7 of its
   total height. A tall, slim figure is wrong, however good it looks on its own.
2. GRID: draw it as a coarse pixel sprite. The character must be exactly 19 logical pixels
   tall, and every logical pixel must be a solid uniform square block at least 40 x 40
   screen pixels in size. Nothing may be smaller than one whole block.
3. COLOUR: flat blocks of colour only, no more than 10 colours in the whole image, and no
   anti-aliasing at all. Every edge must be a hard step between two blocks, never a blend.

CANVAS
- Portrait or square is fine. Roughly 1024 x 1024 is a good size.
- Background fully transparent (alpha 0). If transparency is not possible, fill the entire
  background with solid pure magenta #FF00FF and use that colour nowhere on the character.
- No scenery, no ground, no shadow, no border, no text, no watermark.
- Exactly one character, seen strictly from the FRONT, standing upright, facing the viewer,
  centred horizontally, the whole body inside the canvas with a small margin.

PROPORTIONS - measured from the target game.
Let H be the character's total height, from the top of the hair or headwear to the soles.
Given rule 2, H is 19 logical pixels, so each fraction below is close to a count of blocks.
- TOTAL WIDTH: 0.65 to 0.7 x H, which is 12 to 13 blocks wide. See rule 1.
- Head, including all hair and headwear: from 0.00 H down to 0.50 H - about 10 blocks.
  The head is HALF the total height. The character is 2 heads tall.
- The head is about 0.9 of the total width, nearly as wide as the whole figure.
  The shoulders are the widest part, as wide as the figure itself.
- Eyes: two dark blocks on the band from 0.35 H to 0.45 H. One or two blocks each,
  clearly separated, with at least one block of face between them.
- Torso and upper-body clothing: 0.50 H to 0.85 H.
- Legs, trousers and footwear: 0.85 H to 1.00 H. The legs are short and thick.
  The shirt hangs down as far as 0.85 H, so the band from 0.75 H to 0.85 H is shirt,
  not trousers.
- Arms hang straight down at the sides, close to the body.
  Nothing held, no props, no weapons.

OUTLINE
- A closed dark outline all the way around the character, with no gaps anywhere.
- The outline must be EXACTLY ONE logical pixel thick - one block, nowhere two.
  A thicker outline eats the character once the picture is shrunk.
- Do not outline anything inside the silhouette. No outlines around the shirt, the belt,
  the boots or individual strands of hair.

SILHOUETTE
- The outline of the whole figure must be simple enough to read at a glance.
- No thin spikes anywhere, and that includes the hair. Spiky hair collapses into a solid
  dark blob when the picture is shrunk. Draw hair as one rounded mass, never wider than the
  shoulders - the head with its hair is already about 0.9 of the total width.
- No gaps, holes or floating pieces. Everything is one connected shape.

DO NOT
- No gradients, no shading, no highlights, no texture, no glow, no soft edges.
- No detail smaller than one logical pixel: no buttons, no laces, no stitching, no belt
  holes, no eyebrows, no eye highlights, no teeth, no individual fingers.
- No 3/4 view, no side view, no perspective, no tilt. Strictly front-facing and symmetrical.
- No background of any kind, no frame, no vignette, no drop shadow.
- Do not crop: the whole body from the top of the head to the soles must fit inside the canvas.
- No multiple poses, no turnaround sheet, no colour swatches, no labels.

CHECK BEFORE YOU FINISH
- Is the figure 12 to 13 blocks wide and 19 blocks tall? If it is narrower, widen it.
- Is every edge a hard step, with no blended pixels anywhere?
- Is the outline one block thick everywhere?
- Does the hair read as one rounded mass rather than a set of points?
